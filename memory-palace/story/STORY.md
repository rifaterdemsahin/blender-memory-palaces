# 🎬 The Relay: story v3 (DRAFT, awaiting approval)

Status: ⏳ **Not approved. No film, voiceover or subtitles will be rendered until this story and its storyboard are approved.**

## 🧭 Why this version is different

The earlier films (v1 and v2, see the [changelog](../changelog.html)) told seven separate facts, one per station. Each station was a standalone scene, so the viewer had nothing to link them with. That breaks the memory technique.

The method used here is the **linking (chain) story**, a standard memory technique that the loci-method projects on GitHub also rely on, such as KMind Palace's "AI mnemonic story" that ties each item to its spot with one vivid scene. Here, **one action at each station hands the film to the next station**. The seven items become one cause-and-effect chain:

> The compass needle points → the columns fire an arrow → the arrow carries a camera → the camera is pressed into clay → the clay rolls across the bridge → the glass sphere takes the clay and throws words → the words light the flame → the flame launches the film.

If you forget one link, the chain breaks, and that is what makes the chain easy to rebuild: you ask "what happens next?" at every station.

## 🧩 Rules for every scene

1. **One handoff per station.** The scene ends with an object that moves to the next station.
2. **Exaggerate the object.** Giant, glowing, impossible: a compass the size of a house, a car-length arrow, a bus-sized sphere.
3. **Name the keyword in the action, not just in a label.** The viewer hears it in the story.
4. **One sense per beat plus one motion.** Sight (the image), sound (the narration), motion (the video).
5. **The closing replays the chain in order** and asks the viewer to say it back.

## 📖 Story text (narration)

**S0 · Opening · Overview**
Erdem has one film to make, and seven steps to remember. The steps are a relay. Each step hands the film to the next. Drop one, and the whole film falls.

**S1 · 01 PLAN · Entrance Courtyard**
At the Entrance Courtyard, a golden compass the size of a house slams onto the stone and draws the shot. Plan first: the hero, the setting, the move, the look. Then the needle snaps east, toward the columns.

**S2 · 02 SETTING · Colonnade**
The needle hits the Colonnade. Forty white columns burst out of the ground, and each one holds a world: a mountain, a city, a tree, a wave. Plain grey shapes, at real size. The columns fire a red arrow across the grass, toward the Rotunda.

**S3 · 03 MOVE · Rotunda**
The red arrow, as long as a car, circles the marble ring and trails fire. It stops, and a camera rises from its tail. One camera move, and only one: a dolly, an orbit, a chase, a crane or a truck. The camera rolls off the ring, toward the garden.

**S4 · 04 CLAY · Sunken Garden**
In the Sunken Garden, giant grey figures catch the camera and press the whole shot into clay, fingerprints and all. No colour. If the shape cannot tell the story, nothing can. The figures roll the clay ball out over the bridge.

**S5 · 05 CHECK · Library Bridge**
The clay ball reaches the Library Bridge. Three neon numbers hang over it like judges: one, one hundred and twenty, two hundred and forty. They check every frame. A flaw? The judges shout. Erdem fixes it, and the ball rolls on to the Observatory.

**S6 · 06 PROMPT · Observatory**
In the Observatory, a glass sphere as big as a bus swallows the clay ball. It throws words into the night sky: lock the camera first, then paint the look, in two to four sentences. The words stream down the hill to the altar.

**S7 · 07 RENDER · Summit Altar**
The words land on the Summit Altar, and the flame erupts. A reel of light launches into the sky, ten seconds long, and falls back into Erdem's hands, finished. Seven steps, in order, each one handing the film forward.

**S8 · Closing · Replay**
Now walk it back, watcher. Start at the courtyard. Let the compass point. Hear the columns. Feel the arrow. See the clay, the numbers, the sphere and the flame. Say them out loud, in order: PLAN, SETTING, MOVE, CLAY, CHECK, PROMPT, RENDER. Now remember them.
