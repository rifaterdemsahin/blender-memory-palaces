🧠 Why the memory palace becomes easier

🏛️ The Method of Loci (memory palace) is over 2,000 years old, and it still works. These changes make it easier to use, and here is the reason each one helps.

🔬 1. The science in one minute

🧪 Principle📖 What it means🛠️ Where this palace uses it
🗺️ Spatial memoryThe brain remembers places very well, and order comes naturally along a route.7 stations on one fixed walk.
🤪 DistinctivenessStrange, huge, glowing things stand out from ordinary ones and are recalled more easily.Giant compass, glowing numbers, a flame that fires a film reel.
🎭 Dual codingPairing an image with a word gives the memory two ways to be found again.Each station has a picture 🖼️ and a keyword label 🏷️.
🔊 Multiple sensesHearing and seeing the same idea strengthens the trace.Each station has a narration 🎙️ as well as the image and video.
🎬 MotionMoving scenes are more memorable than still ones.A 5-second video 🎬 per station and a 10-second walkthrough.
🔁 Retrieval practicePulling a memory out, rather than re-reading it, is what makes it stick.The walkthrough and the modal pop-ups are built for active recall 🧪.
🪜 ChunkingSeven items is a small, manageable number.Exactly 7 stations, each with one item.

🛠️ 2. What changed to make it easier

  • 🧭 A fixed route: the same 7 stations in the same order every time, so the route never changes.
  • 🪟 One tap to a station: each station opens a pop-up with its picture, video and narration.
  • 🏷️ Readable labels: the palace labels now face the walkthrough camera, so the words read correctly from any side.
  • 🎙️ Narration per station: you hear the keyword and its meaning, so you can test yourself with your eyes closed.
  • 🔗 Links between stations: each pop-up links to the next station, so the story flows.
  • 🧩 The same palace in two styles: a coloured memory version and a plain clay version for practice 🧱.

✅ 3. How to practise (5 minutes)

  1. 🏠 Open the palace and tap station 01 · 🏷️ PLAN.
  2. 👀 Look at the picture and listen to the narration once.
  3. 🙈 Close the pop-up, close your eyes, and walk to station 01 in your head.
  4. 🗣️ Say the keyword out loud before you look again.
  5. 🔁 Repeat for all seven, then go back to the start and say them in order.

⚠️ 4. What this does not claim

🔬 The principles above are well established in memory research. A specific gain in your own recall still has to be measured by you, by testing yourself over several days.
