# 50-credit daily planner (Google Flow, free account)

Google gives every free account 50 Flow credits a day. They don't roll over, so use them or lose them.

## What things cost (Omni Flash, Google's credits page)
| What | Cost |
|---|---|
| 1 second at 360p | 1 credit |
| 1 second at 720p | 3 credits |
| A 10 s test at 360p | 10 credits |
| A 10 s final at 720p | 30 credits |
| A 5 s final at 720p | 15 credits |

Clips must be 3 to 10 seconds on the free plan.

## Plans for one day
| Plan | Spend | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **The safe day** (recommended) | 2 tests at 360p (20) + 1 final at 720p (30) = 50 | one finished 10 s shot, with one retry to fix the prompt |
| **The explorer** | 5 tests at 360p (50) | try 5 looks on the same clip, pick tomorrow's final |
| **Two short finals** | 1 test at 360p (10) + 2 finals of 6 s at 720p (36) = 46 | two 6 s shots for a sequence |
| **The trailer week** | one safe day per shot | 5 days = 5 finished shots = a 50 s trailer for $0 |

## Rules that save credits
1. Always test at 360p first. You see the look and how the camera moves, for a third of the price.
2. Change one thing per retry, either the prompt or the Blender scene, never both at once.
3. Fix shapes in Blender for free, not by spending credits on retries in Flow.
4. Trim your clip to the part you need. 6 good seconds cost less than 10.
5. Free accounts can be blocked from video during busy hours (roughly 13:00 to 16:00 UTC). Plan your Flow work outside them.
