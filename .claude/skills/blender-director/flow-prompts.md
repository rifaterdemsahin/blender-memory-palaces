# 10 Flow prompt templates (Omni Flash, video-to-video)

How to use: upload your grey clip, choose Omni Flash, paste one template, change the words in [brackets]. Keep it to 2-4 sentences. The first sentence is the same in every template: it tells Flow to keep your Blender camera move.

**Camera lock (first line of every prompt):**
Keep the exact camera path, framing, timing and object positions from this video; only change how everything looks.

## If the result drifts
- The camera moved differently: make the prompt shorter. Keep line 1 plus one short sentence of look.
- Something appeared that isn't in your scene: fill that empty area in Blender and render again.
- Two objects swapped or merged: make them different sizes or shapes in Blender.
- Wrong shape (a bent limb, a flat wheel): correct it in Blender. Flow copies the shapes it's given.
